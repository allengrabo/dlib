# 1Wave Concept — Single Continuous Curved Flex-LED Wall

## Use case
- stylized-concept

## Asset type
- Architectural concept render for stakeholder presentation

## Primary request
Create a high-end architectural visualization of the **1Wave** installation as **one continuous monolithic flex-LED wall** in an airport lobby.

## Core geometry and scale (must be accurate)
- Overall LED wall size: **60 feet long x 12 feet high**
- Panelization: **36 columns x 7 rows** of LED panels across the full surface
- The panel seams should be subtle and read as fabrication joints only, while the whole surface still reads as one uninterrupted canvas
- The wall curves in **plan view** as a **single smooth wave profile**
- Curvature behavior from left to right:
  - Left third: relatively calm, close to flat
  - Middle: gradually increasing bend
  - Right third: noticeably stronger curvature
- No segmentation into fins, stacked bands, shelves, or separated strips
- The top and bottom edges should follow the same continuous wall body; no offset layers

## Visual content on LED surface
- Display abstract **blue-white water caustic** visuals
- Brightness and contrast tuned for a premium indoor public interior
- Add subtle localized interaction ripples around nearby people (small circular or elliptical distortions in the caustic pattern)
- Keep the effect elegant and understated, not playful or cartoonish

## Environment (airport lobby)
- Bright, premium contemporary airport interior
- Polished reflective floor
- Clean white ceiling with linear recessed lighting
- Structural columns visible
- Queue stanchions / guidance posts visible in foreground or mid-ground
- Simplified human figures for scale and occupancy
- Maintain realistic circulation clearances around the wall

## Camera and composition
- Eye-level to slightly elevated wide-angle architectural view
- Show enough perspective to clearly read the plan curvature transition from calm-left to dynamic-right
- Frame the entire 60-foot wall within the shot if possible
- Avoid extreme distortion; architectural realism preferred

## Material and lighting
- LED face: glossy-to-semi-gloss emissive surface
- Surrounding architectural finishes: clean neutral palette (whites, light grays, metallic accents)
- Daylit + artificial lighting balance; bright but controlled highlights
- Floor reflections should subtly mirror the blue-white LED glow

## Explicit constraints (negative prompts)
- Do **not** split the installation into separate horizontal ribbons or stacked strips
- Do **not** add protruding black fins, blades, louvers, or shelf-like elements
- Do **not** break the wall into disconnected sections
- Do **not** turn this into a tunnel, portal, or wraparound room
- Do **not** use warm color palettes on the LED content; keep to blue-white caustic tones
- Do **not** place heavy branding, signage, or text on the LED wall

## Single-shot generation prompt
"Photorealistic architectural concept render of a premium airport lobby featuring one continuous monolithic flex-LED media wall called 1Wave, exactly 60 feet long and 12 feet high, built from a 36 by 7 panel grid with subtle seams, the entire wall curving in plan view as one smooth wave that starts nearly flat on the left and increases to stronger curvature on the right, no segmented ribbons and no fins, one uninterrupted LED canvas. The LED surface displays abstract luminous blue-white water caustic patterns with subtle localized interaction ripples around nearby visitors. Environment includes polished reflective floor, white ceiling with linear recessed lights, structural columns, queue stanchions, and simplified human figures for scale. Bright premium contemporary interior, realistic materials, clean composition, architectural visualization quality, wide-angle but natural perspective, full wall visible, crisp detail, high dynamic range, no text, no logo." 

## Optional refinement pass prompt
"Keep the same camera and layout. Increase continuity so the LED wall reads as one single body, reduce any appearance of horizontal banding, and make left-side curvature calmer while right-side curvature becomes more pronounced. Preserve blue-white caustic content and subtle people-driven ripple interactions."
